# GIS-Based Distribution Network Modeling

**Role:** Power System Modeling Consultant (Remote)  
**Organization:** Bangladesh Power Development Board (BPDB)  
**Period:** April 2024 – July 2024  
**Tool:** ETAP

## Scope
Modeled and analyzed a GIS-based distribution network covering 11 kV and 33 kV systems, including 15 feeders, more than 650 buses, and over 3,000 loads.

## Work performed
- Integrated GIS-based infrastructure data to build the electrical network model.
- Performed load-flow, short-circuit, and arc-flash analysis.
- Reviewed relay coordination, voltage regulation, and load-loss performance.
- Evaluated network behavior and identified areas for corrective action.

## Engineering relevance
The project combined electrical power-system fundamentals with network data preparation and simulation to support distribution-system analysis and reliability assessment.
